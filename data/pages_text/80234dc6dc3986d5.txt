Yes No Business · 1 min. read · 1 reads · 30 Sep 2026 30 Sep 2026 DICEUS CEO Illia Pinchuk on the RiskCellar podcast DICEUS CEO Illia Pinchuk joined Brandon Schuh and Nick Hartmann on the RiskCellar podcast to discuss how AI is changing insurance software and what this means for brokers, MGAs, carriers, and captives. The conversation focused on a practical question: how can insurance businesses make their operations faster and less expensive to change? Illia and the hosts explored several shifts: From per-seat pricing to value- and outcome-based models. The discussion looked at how software pricing can reflect the value delivered to an insurance business. From complex technology stacks to more connected workflows. Reducing friction across legacy systems remains an important part of improving operations. From lengthy changes to configurable platforms. End-to-end platforms can help businesses adapt workflows faster as their needs evolve. From AI adoption to controlled enterprise use. Clear controls, traceability, and audit trails are essential when introducing AI into insurance operations. The episode also covered how insurance organizations can scale without a proportional increase in software costs and operational complexity.
