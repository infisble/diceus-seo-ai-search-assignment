Yes No Company news · 2 min. read · 1106 reads · 26 Mar 2025 24 Sep 2026 DICEUS attended Insurtech Insights Europe 2025 On March 19-20, the DICEUS team, Illia Pinchuk, Kateryna Monastyrska, and Dmytro Kazymir, attended Europe’s largest insurtech conference, Insurtech Insights Europe 2025, in London. CEO Illia Pinchuk showcased our Super App developed for insurance businesses to reduce operational costs, increase revenue, and improve customer loyalty. Presenting five use cases for health, life (unit-linked), and car insurance, Illia demonstrated how the app could benefit each. The examples were only a selection of 250+ use cases driving innovation in the insurance sector. Speaking about the benefits of our Super App, Illia distinguished the following: Self-service tools can reduce call center costs Seamless digital workflows accelerate claims processing Fund rebalancing optimizes unit-linked insurance operations Event-based recommendations unlock upselling and cross-selling opportunities Operational overhead can be reduced if customers have self-service options Our team is grateful to our partners and organizers for providing us with this incredible opportunity to introduce our software products and connect with so many forward-thinking professionals. Looking forward to continued collaboration and the exciting events ahead!
