Yes No Company news · 2 min. read · 274 reads · 26 Jan 2026 26 Jan 2026 DICEUS at Embedded Insurance Symposium in Zurich On January 20, DICEUS CEO Illia Pinchuk and Head of Insurance Technology Solutions Kateryna Monastyrska visited a “Embedded Insurance: Ecosystems, Innovation and Growth” symposium hosted at ETH Zurich in Zurich. It was organized by our partner, the Swiss Insurtech Hub, for insurance executives, insurtech companies, and innovation leaders to explore the future of embedded insurance. The event featured special guests from Allianz Partners, Zurich Insurance, Generali, and Wolt. The keynote of the symposium — embedded insurance today is a technology problem, not a marketing one. Data warehouses, clean data, a clear data strategy, modern technologies, artificial intelligence, and execution speed determine who scales—and it is precisely where DICEUS can help. For DICEUS, being at ETH Zurich was symbolic for one more reason: it is where technology, research, and future engineers meet. We believe that developing technology is, first of all, about developing people. Our team actively invests in early talent and internship programs. Beyond that, we see strong potential to work with professors, lecturers, and students from ETH Zurich on applied research and real-world insurtech use cases. In addition to attending the symposium, we also visited Groupe Mutuel at its office in Lausanne. We presented our ready-made software products and discussed how our solutions can support digital transformation in insurance.
