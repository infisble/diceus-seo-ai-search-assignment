Yes No Company news · 2 min. read · 69 reads · 10 Jun 2026 10 Jun 2026 DICEUS joins VCIA as a technology partner DICEUS has partnered with the Vermont Captive Insurance Association (VCIA), the world’s largest captive insurance trade association, to give their 400+ member organizations direct access to purpose-built software that includes Captive Management Platform and Captive Owner Portal. Today, most captive managers still do their work across disconnected spreadsheets and outdated software systems. DICEUS changes that for VCIA members with its captive technology suite: Captive Management Platform with many capabilities for captive managers: policy administration, financials, claim management, reinsurance, and compliance and reporting. Captive Owner Portal offers complete visibility for captive owners: captive performance overview, premiums, claims, solvency, and financial health in a single portal with in-built AI-driven insights. DICEUS CEO Illia Pinchuk says: “Captive managers have been working around legacy software solutions for too long. DICEUS partnership with VCIA offers their members access to dedicated software – from policy administration and financials to a real-time owner portal. That’s a meaningful shift for the industry.” Our team is excited to join the VCIA community and committed to making captive processes simpler, more transparent, and more efficient for both captive managers and captive owners.
