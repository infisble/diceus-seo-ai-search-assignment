Business challenge Velaw set out to transform and future-proof its compliance and risk management platform for financial services providers. The previous system had reached its limits – constraining scalability, customization, and the rollout of new capabilities. Velaw and DICEUS joined forces to build a next-generation, state-of-the-art RegTech solution. Designed for agility and constant evolution, the new VELA platform delivers full alignment with client requirements, business objectives, and regulatory expectations, while it remains ready to integrate emerging technologies and innovations as they arise. Technical challenges The project’s success depended on deep understanding of the Velaw’s business processes and compliance logic. The team had to design a new, scalable software architecture aligned with business workflows, ensure seamless data migration from the legacy system, and create a secure, future-ready platform to host new functionalities. Solution delivered We developed an MVP for the new VELA RegTech platform, built entirely on a modern technology stack – Python for backend and Angular for frontend. The solution achieves significantly improved performance, scalability, and maintainability while mirroring the client’s updated compliance and risk management workflows. VELA delivers end-to-end compliance coverage, a user-driven interface, and a secure, modular architecture. Its clean codebase and open structure make it ready for future analytics and built-in interactive reporting – the next phase of VELA’s digital evolution.
