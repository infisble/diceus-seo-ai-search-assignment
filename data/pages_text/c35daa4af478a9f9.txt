Yes No Press releases · 2 min. read · 540 reads · 30 May 2024 05 Mar 2026 DICEUS joined Insurtech UK as an Associate Member May 28, 2024, Vienna, Austria – DICEUS is proud to announce its new role as an Associate Member of Insurtech UK, a large and growing ecosystem representing over 100 insurtech companies. Insurteck UK is dedicated to transforming the insurance sector with technological advancements. By combining the expertise of numerous insurtechs and insurers, Insurtech UK gives a unique opportunity to connect, collaborate, and exchange knowledge. DICEUS joined Insurtech UK to present and offer its software products that automate key insurance processes and improve customer experience. These products include a mobile app, chatbot, web portal, customer feedback tool, and data analytics. By implementing DICEUS solutions, insurers can offer 24/7 online customer support and many self-service capabilities like claim submitting, appointment scheduling, buying insurance policies, and uploading doctor’s reports and other relevant documents. “We are proud to join Insurtech UK as an Associate Member and hope our solutions will help insurance companies expand digital capabilities for their customers. Today, insurance consumers want to report claims online or make an appointment with a doctor without calling medical assistance. Our ready-made software makes that possible,” said DICEUS CEO Illia Pinchuk. “We are happy to welcome DICEUS to our ecosystem. I believe that our products will significantly enhance the digital capabilities of our member insurers,” said Melissa Collett, CEO of Insurtech UK. About DICEUS DICEUS offers innovative technological solutions and end-to-end IT services for insurance companies to improve customer experience and engagement, automate operations, and increase revenue in the A&H and P&C lines of business. About Insurtech UK Insurtech UK is a leading community of technology-driven companies transforming the insurance industry. It is a collaborative platform for insurtechs, insurers, partners, and investors. By fostering innovation and facilitating industry partnerships, Insurtech UK aims to drive efficiency, enhance customer experiences, and shape the future of insurance.
