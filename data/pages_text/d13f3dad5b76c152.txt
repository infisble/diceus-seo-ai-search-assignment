Yes No Company news · 2 min. read · 358 reads · 02 Feb 2026 04 Feb 2026 DICEUS CEO Illia Pinchuk pitched at ITC London 2026 On January 26-27, our CEO, Illia Pinchuk, Head of Insurance Technology Solutions, Kateryna Monastyrska, and Head of Growth, Dmytro Kazymir, attended ITC London 2026 to represent DICEUS. Illia Pinchuk spoke about why it’s important to modernize policy administration systems. He shared how insurers can move away from legacy systems more quickly and affordably by updating them step by step instead of replacing everything at once. Our team also had productive talks with teams from top insurance companies like Groupe Mutuel, Aviva, AXA, Swiss Re, ERGO, UNIQA, VIG, Dai-ichi Life, and KI Insurance. We discussed upcoming projects for 2026–2027, along with the actual challenges and timelines involved. We were glad to catch up with our friends Gilles Bournier, Thomas Darbellay, Silvia Signoretti, and Sharan Kaur. Here are the main things we learned from the event: Artificial intelligence can’t move forward without strong data foundations and better connections between policies, claims, and underwriting systems. The industry is shifting from demos and pilot projects to real solutions that fit into existing systems and show clear results. Automation in underwriting, policy administration, and claims only works when the backend and frontend are designed to function together as one system. This is exactly what we do at DICEUS — building core backends, frontends, and customer journeys that work together as one system.
