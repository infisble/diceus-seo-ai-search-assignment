The event provided insightful views on the changing insurtech landscape, ranging from capacity issues and global expansion plans to scaling solutions and how AI transforms insurance. As a vendor of pre-built insurance software solutions, we were particularly struck by discussions of automating customer engagement and data analytics segments in which our offerings are already creating tangible results for insurers globally. Highlights of the event included the session on “Regulating AI Around the World” and the launch of the FCA’s new center for live AI testing. These events highlighted our opportunities and obligations as innovators in this rapidly developing area. Dmytro was glad to be reunited with our Insurtech UK partners, Deloitte and KPMG colleagues, and other market leaders.
