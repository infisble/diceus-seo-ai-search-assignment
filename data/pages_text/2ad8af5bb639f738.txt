Yes No Company news · 1 min. read · 142 reads · 23 Mar 2026 24 Mar 2026 DICEUS DWH for Insurance has been named one of the 2026 FinTech Breakthrough Award winners We’re excited to share that DICEUS Data Warehouse for Insurance has been named Predictive Analytics Platform of the Year by FinTech Breakthrough. This independent organization honors the most innovative companies and solutions in global financial technology each year. This award highlights our team’s work in building a strong, enterprise-level data warehouse designed for insurance companies. It brings together scattered operational data into one reliable source. With features like real-time data ingestion, compliance reporting, BI dashboards, and predictive analytics, DICEUS DWH helps insurers make better, faster decisions using data, no matter their size. Each year, the FinTech Breakthrough Awards review thousands of nominations in many financial technology categories. Winning this award puts DICEUS among the top innovators shaping the future of financial services around the world. This award not only recognizes our achievements so far but also inspires us to keep improving. We’re committed to making our insurance data infrastructure even stronger, growing our BI features, and making analytics easier to use for insurance organizations of every size. Thank you to our clients, partners, and everyone at DICEUS for making this achievement possible.
