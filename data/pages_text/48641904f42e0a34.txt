Business challenge We needed to provide an easy-to-use and convenient solution to gather visual information in real time and analyze it to observe the Earth. It would help to quickly get relevant information on any required area and control its state. We aimed to enable access to geospatial and satellite capabilities for the most accurate results. Technical challenges Cloud-based software helps simplify and automate many underlying tasks and make the work much more manageable. Now, the staff can get statistics and all the necessary information in a few minutes. It helps to compare the state of the area before and after various events such as natural disasters or pollutions. Solution delivered In 11 months, DICEUS team has developed a cloud-based solution to observe the Earth with satellites’ images. We aimed to ease the exploring process and get access to geospatial images. The solution helps to quickly get actual information of any area and analyze it according to all the standards. We can get a detailed analysis of any zone and how nature or human processes affected it. It allows making quick comparisons of images of different periods.
