Yes No Press releases · 2 min. read · 558 reads · 24 May 2024 05 Mar 2026 DICEUS becomes a member of the Swiss InsurTech Hub May 20, 2024. Vienna, Austria–DICEUS, an insurance software development company, has established partnership relationships with the Swiss InsurTech Hub, a non-profit association promoting the digitization and innovation of the insurance sector. Since 2011, DICEUS has developed many solutions for insurance and insurtech companies like UNIQA, Kniazha VIG, WTW, BriteCore, Fadata, and more. Recently, DICEUS introduced its family of software products for the insurance industry, which includes five products: a mobile app, chatbot, web portal, data analytics, and customer feedback tool. All these solutions aim to help insurance companies digitize and automate their processes, improve customer experience, and use valuable data insights for better decision-making. Understanding the unity of purposes with the Swiss InsurTech Hub, DICEUS CEO Illia Pinchuk has decided to collaborate with this organization to make DICEUS a part of the SIH ecosystem. “I think that the Swiss InsurTech Hub offers a good opportunity for insurtech providers to connect with other organizations in the industry, share our knowledge, and showcase DICEUS’s expertise in insurance digital products development,” – Illia Pinchuk says. “We are happy DICEUS has joined our association as they share our mission in promoting innovation and digitization in the insurance sector. I belive their expertise and knowledge of the insurtech solutions will add value to our community and ecosystem,” – the President of the Swiss InsurTech Hub Silvia Signoretti says. About DICEUS DICEUS offers innovative technological solutions and end-to-end IT services for insurance companies to improve customer experience and engagement, automate operations, and increase revenue in the A&H and P&C lines of business. About the Swiss InsurTech Hub The Swiss InsurTech Hub is a non-profit association that promotes the digitization and innovation of the insurance sector and supports the exchange of knowledge developed by insurtech companies.
