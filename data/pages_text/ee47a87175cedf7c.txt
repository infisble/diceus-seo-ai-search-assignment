Yes No Company news · 2 min. read · 639 reads · 24 Mar 2025 24 Mar 2025 DICEUS CEO Illia Pinchuk was a speaker at Insurtech UK Member Pitching Session On March 17, DICEUS CEO Illia Pinchuk attended a Member Pitching Session organized by Insurtech UK in partnership with Aon at The Leadenhall Building, London. Illia shared DICEUS expertise in digital channels for insurance providers—specifically, how mobile applications can reduce costs, increase revenue, and enhance customer loyalty. He presented five use cases demonstrating how digital transformation benefits Life (Unit-Linked), Health, and Car insurance products. These are just a fraction of the 250+ use cases we’ve developed at DICEUS to drive efficiency and innovation in the insurance industry. Key takeaways: Reduce call center costs with AI-powered self-service tools Accelerate claims processing through seamless digital workflows Optimize fund rebalancing for Unit-Linked insurance Unlock upselling and cross-selling opportunities using event-based recommendations Empower customers with self-service options, reducing operational overhead We are grateful to Insurtech UK and CEO Melissa Collett for organizing such a fantastic event and to Martyn Denney, Head of Innovation & Investments, AON UK, for hosting it. Our team appreciates Edward Allanson’s insightful feedback and fresh perspectives (McKinsey & Company) and Sokhiba Mukhitdinova’s thoughtful questions. If you’re seeking ways to optimize your customer experience, operations, and revenue, contact us to explore our software solutions.
