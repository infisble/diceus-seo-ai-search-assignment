Yes No Company news · 1 min. read · 509 reads · 13 Nov 2024 08 Apr 2026 DICEUS Team at Insurance Innovators Summit in London Recently, our team, Illia Pinchuk, DICEUS CEO, and Kateryna Monastyrska, Head of Marketing and Sales, visited Insurance Innovators Summit in London. The event united over 1500 professionals from the insurance industry who discussed key trends in the sector and shared their experiences. We were glad to meet with industry leaders like AXA, Allianz, Hiscox, Generali, VIG, Aviva, WTW, Tokio Marine, Sava Insurance Group, and many others to introduce our ready-made software products that include a Super App, LLM-based chatbot, web portal, embedded insurance widget, DWH and data analytics. One of the central topics at the summit was the importance of technology in providing an excellent customer experience and building customer loyalty through digital interactions. Insights on this topic were emphasized by David McMillan, CEO of Esure Group, Mark Klein, Chief Digital Officer at ERGO, Mariel Vazquez, Customer Experience Director at MAPFRE, and Magda Ramada, Senior Director at WTW. Illia and Kateryna were assured of the benefits that DICEUS software products bring to insurance – convenient CX, improved customer satisfaction, and reduced operational costs.
