Find the right solution for your needs Talk to our expert Illia Pinchuk CEO and Founder of DICEUS FAQ What are alternative risk solutions? Alternative Risk Transfer (ART) solutions are non‑traditional ways to administer risks. These risk management strategies help businesses transfer and finance risks outside the conventional insurance market. Examples of these strategies include captive insurance, risk retention groups, self‑insurance, risk pooling, and more. What software is used to administer alternative risks? Traditional software used in the insurance industry to manage conventional policies doesn't fit the needs of organizations managing alternative risks. These companies need specialized software that includes functionalities to manage policy lifecycle, track and process claims, manage actuarial and financial processes, do reporting and analytics, and more. How does DICEUS platform differ from that we already use? Homegrown systems in this space typically cover one or two workflows well. The gap is usually in multi‑entity reporting or reconciliation when a new bordereau format shows up. The platform can sit alongside what you've built rather than replacing it outright. Why is DICEUS AROP better than most generic insurance software? Most systems were built for a single carrier book, not for administering multiple entities or programs at once. Our platform covers the multi‑entity and delegated‑authority reporting pieces that were typically added as separate features elsewhere. Our programs, cells, or client requirements are all different. Won't a standard system force us into one template? No. The platform is built around configuration per entity – not one template applied to all of them. Rules, chart of accounts, and reporting formats are set once per entity, which is the direct answer to structural heterogeneity across a book.
