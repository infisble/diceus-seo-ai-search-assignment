Yes No Company news · 2 min. read · 889 reads · 26 Jun 2025 26 Jun 2025 DICEUS has been recognized by Microsoft and accepted into the Founders Hub We’re proud to share that DICEUS has been officially recognized by Microsoft and accepted into the Microsoft Startups Founders Hub — a highly selective global program created to empower the most innovative and high-potential companies. This milestone is especially meaningful because our CEO, Illia Pinchuk, personally led and submitted the application — a testament to his hands-on leadership and the vision that continues to drive our growth. Microsoft evaluates each applicant with care, and not every company makes the cut. That’s why this recognition means so much to us — it’s a strong validation of our product, our vision, and the real value we’re delivering to the insurance industry and beyond. Microsoft Startup Founders Hub Program provides us with the opportunity to access Microsoft’s AI tools and infrastructure, as well as exclusive advantages and Azure cloud credits, to build our products. Our teams are gaining access to Microsoft’s AI products, including models such as OpenAI GPT-3.5 Turbo and GPT-4, which are backed by Azure’s security. It will enable us to address our customers’ challenges with innovative AI solutions backed by 24/7 Azure Support, an Expert Network, and a subscription to GitHub Enterprise. As a member of the Microsoft Startup Founders Hub, DICEUS uses the widest range of Microsoft services, including Microsoft Power Platform, Dynamics 365, and Visual Studio Enterprise. By joining the program, DICEUS can now leverage Microsoft’s full technology stack to build smarter, faster, and more scalable solutions. These resources empower our teams to create innovative, AI-driven products tailored to client needs, backed by expert support and robust cloud capabilities. We’re grateful for the recognition and excited to take full advantage of the opportunities ahead.
