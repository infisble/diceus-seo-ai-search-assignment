Yes No Company news · 2 min. read · 178 reads · 09 Mar 2026 04 Sep 2026 DICEUS attended the Airmic Captives Forum in London On March 4, the DICEUS Team visited the Airmic Captives Forum in London. Our CEO, Illia Pinchuk, and Head of Insurance Technology Solutions, Kateryna Monastyrska, joined captive leaders, risk professionals, and industry experts for strategic discussions on the future of captive insurance. The Airmic Captives Forum brings together the captive community to exchange perspectives on how captives evolve as a core risk-financing instrument. One highlight of the event was the presentation of the 2026 Airmic Captives Survey Report, delivered by Airmic’s CEO, Julia Graham, with Oliver Davies and Paul Eaton. The findings show how captives are increasingly used not only as alternative risk transfer vehicles but as strategic instruments for organizational resilience. Among the topics discussed at the forum were the growing complexity of the global risk landscape, encompassing geopolitical instability and regulatory change, as well as climate exposure and evolving liability risks. This reinforced the role of captives as an important factor in modern risk financing strategies. The forum also covered progress toward a dedicated UK captive insurance framework, highlighting strong industry and regulator engagement. To summarize, the event showed the evolving role of captives in risk management and enterprise resilience. The event stressed the essential role of collaboration between industry leaders, regulators, and risk professionals as the landscape continues to shift.
