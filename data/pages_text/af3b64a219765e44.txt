What is the difference between rule-based and AI-driven chatbots in chatbot development services? Rule-based chatbots function according to pre-defined rules and algorithms that map out conversations and can be altered only by developers who introduce changes to them. AI-fueled chatbots rely on machine learning techniques, which means they can understand the intent and context of a question before answering it and modify their responses depending on the information they collect.
