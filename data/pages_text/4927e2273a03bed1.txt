Yes No Company news · 1 min. read · 515 reads · 04 Mar 2024 05 Mar 2026 DICEUS Team attended the “Insurance Innovators Fraud & Claims” event in London We are happy to inform that the DICEUS team, represented by Illia Pinchuk, CEO of DICEUS, and Kateryna Monastyrska, Head of Sales and Marketing, had the pleasure of attending Europe’s leading claims and fraud event, “Insurance Innovators Fraud & Claims,” in London on February 27. During the event, we had insightful discussions with leading insurance companies from across Europe and Great Britain about the technologies that can be used to predict and prevent fraud, as well as the future of digital claims processing. We also had the opportunity to introduce the DICEUS family of next-generation CX automation software which consists of five products (mobile app, chatbot, web portal, analytics, and customer feedback tool) specifically developed for the insurance sector. We are grateful to the event organizers for bringing together such a wealth of expertise and look forward to further engaging with the insurance business leaders.
