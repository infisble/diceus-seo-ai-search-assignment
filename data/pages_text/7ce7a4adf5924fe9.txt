Business challenge Fadata is growing and scaling up consultancy, building a more professional setting for support, and developing a digital learning and training experience for partners and customers. As a result, Fadata was looking for reliable partners that could be subcontractors or implementation partners when they reached a certain level. With partners, Fadata can fulfill all the customers’ implementation requests. Technical challenges Fadata’s product is very complex, so it takes a lot of time for our people to understand all the details and to figure out where to look when they have a task or a bug fix that needs to be done. For this collaboration, knowing the technology is not enough; they also need to understand the insurance domain and learn how Fadata’s product INSIS, which is rather complex, works. That is why our software engineers go through a comprehensive onboarding program, need to watch a lot of training videos, and read a lot of documentation. Moreover, they also perform some customer-facing activities, which is rare for IT roles. Solution delivered Three developers and a business consultant were provided from our side to work for Fadata Professional Services Department. Additional resources will help Fadata improve and speed up the solution implementation processes. In addition, having a reliable implementation partner positively affects Fadata’s relationships with its clients.
