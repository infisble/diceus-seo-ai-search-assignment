Yes No Company news · 2 min. read · 2202 reads · 02 Aug 2021 24 Sep 2026 DICEUS is opening a new DICEUS Nordics office in Denmark We are happy to announce that DICEUS has opened a new DICEUS Nordics office in Denmark. Its key function will be to support our sales and business development activities across five countries: Denmark, Norway, Sweden, Finland, and Iceland. The office started its work on June 1, and as of today, we have formalized its growth and development plan, strategy, and budget for the next few years. The DICEUS Nordics office is currently represented in Denmark by its CEO, Poul Ørum. Please find more information about the team below. Poul Ørum is a trained civil engineer with 20-year experience in software engineering for the insurance industry. Poul has been a C-level executive in several Danish software development companies. Today, Poul is an owner and partner in RiskVille, supplying cloud solutions for insurance management. Illia Pinchuk, the DICEUS CEO, says about his expectations of the new office: “With 10 years of international IT experience and more than 100 accomplished projects, we’ve developed an effective software development practice oriented on results. Thanks to that, we plan to broaden our geography by making our customers from Nordics more technology-driven and successful!” In his turn, Poul Ørum, the DICEUS Nordics CEO, admitted: “In the Nordic region, there is a very strong demand for experienced IT professionals. We aim to help Danish, Norwegian and Swedish companies solve their urgent requests for competencies and fill the gaps. We expect to have at least 4-5 happy customers by the end of 2021.” The new office is expected to generate sales of custom software engineering and IT staff augmentation services provided by DICEUS.
