Business challenge Ricardo Resendiz, Wongleer author and founder, worked as an attorney for over 18 years. Now, he has his own law firm and clearly understands the common challenge of connecting with clients and partners. Especially when it comes to international relationships. To solve these key issues for a scope of industries, Resendiz conceived a unique project – a social networking system tailored to business needs. It had to connect companies effectively, let them find clients, and promote their professional expertise. We were happy to help Resendiz to design this project. Technical challenges In the core of Wongleer, we implemented a special “robot” tool responsible for data scrapping and profile creation. Another major feature we focused on was the client recommendation logic – a special algorithm that helps find potential clients based on the in-depth analysis of an extensive set of parameters. The accuracy of its results depends on the completion of user profile fields – the more user info Wongleer has, the more correct and relevant recommendations it provides. Solution delivered Briefly, we developed a web solution for high-performance business networking. Wongleer works like LinkedIn and Facebook combined. It allows companies to fill profiles, add core info, specify demands. Then, the custom algorithm suggests the most suitable partners and sends leads. Businesses can also search for other companies independently, message them, and initiate cooperation. Wongleer also has business content promotion tools. Moreover, we are currently working on the next version of the platform. Several major upgrades for even more powerful networking are under development.
