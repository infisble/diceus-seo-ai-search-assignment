Yes No Press releases · 2 min. read · 542 reads · 10 Jun 2024 05 Mar 2026 DICEUS starts its partnership journey with Claim Technology DICEUS has started its partnership journey with Claim Technology to build and maintain integrations for DICEUS’s digital products within the Claim Technology Insurtech Marketplace. DICEUS family of five software products for insurance companies automate key insurance processes and improve customer engagement. The first product that will be integrated into Claim Technology’s insurtech marketplace is Customer Feedback Tool. This solution enables companies to rapidly get customer feedback and reviews through customizable surveys after any customer interaction (claim submission, policy purchase, communication with medical assistance operator, etc.). The request to leave feedback is sent to customers via email, SMS, or other channels like Claim Technology’s self-serve chatbot, for instance. An insurance company can ask clients to share their reviews on social media. Clients get specialized offerings, discounts, or other personalized gifts in return for providing feedback. “The Customer Feedback Tool has helped our clients get a helicopter view of customer satisfaction and automate feedback publishing. In addition, the tool allows insurers to promote their products and service through positive reviews on social media and encourage more people to become their customers,” says DICEUS CEO Illia Pinchuk. “By adding a customer feedback loop inside self-serve journeys created using Claim Technology’s no-code tooling, platform users will get a comprehensive view of customer satisfaction, automate review generation procedure, and be able to use data gathered for adjusting their cross/up-selling strategies and marketing efforts,” says Michael Lewis CEO, Claim Technology: About DICEUS DICEUS offers innovative technological solutions and end-to-end IT services for insurance companies to improve customer experience and engagement, automate operations, and increase revenue in the A&H and P&C lines of business. About Claim Technology Claim Technology helps insurers, MGAs, TPAs and Brokers re-imagine claims. Our no-code/low-code design studio, cloud-apps and insurtech APIs empower operations and IT teams to rapidly automate in the cloud, with plugins to a marketplace of 70 AI insurtechs.
