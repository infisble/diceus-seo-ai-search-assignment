Yes No Business · Insurance · 1 min. read · 51 reads · 29 Jun 2026 30 Jun 2026 DICEUS CEO Illia Pinchuk joined Insurance Nerds’ Profiles in Risk podcast Our CEO, Illia Pinchuk, joined Tony Canas on the Profiles in Risk podcast by Insurance Nerds to talk about how insurance technology is changing. The conversation covered three shifts reshaping the industry: From custom builds to product-led SaaS. Insurers are moving away from one-off custom software toward configurable, SaaS-based platforms. From manual underwriting to AI-supported decisions. AI is taking on submission intake, risk assessment, and portfolio-level decisions that used to depend on manual review. From enterprise-software dominance to room for specialists. Large platforms no longer meet the insurance business needs — specialized insurance technology providers now have a real opening. Illia also talked about what it’s like working across the spectrum of the industry — insurers, MGAs, brokers, captives, and captive managers — and why AI is opening up new ways for businesses to access and manage risks.
