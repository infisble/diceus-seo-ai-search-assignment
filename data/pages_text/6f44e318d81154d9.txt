Yes No Company news · 1 min. read · 296 reads · 12 Nov 2025 04 Sep 2026 DICEUS at European Captive Forum 2025 On November 5-6, Olexiy Denysenko , Business Development Manager at DICEUS, visited European Captive Forum in Luxembourg. European Captive Forum is a leading networking and educational event that gathers captive owners, captive managers, domiciles, and those who serve and advise the captive insurance community in Europe. The forum provided attendees with a great opportunity to listen to speakers from top companies in the field: Aon, WTW, Allianz Commercial, AXA XL, Generali, Munich Re, and many more. The central thought of the event lies in the fact that many captives still use siloed systems for policy administration, claims management, and billing, often relying on manual reconciliations and endless spreadsheets. The data silos slow reporting, data visibility, and decision making.
