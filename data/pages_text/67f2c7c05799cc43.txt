Products The product management module allows insurers to create and manage insurance products with precision and scalability in mind. The system provides a clear hierarchical view of active, draft, deactivated, or archived insurance product families, enabling insurers to create root or sub‑products easily. Each product can be configured to meet the particular laws, regulations, and markets, and according to coverage types, custom fields, and multiple versions, if required. Administrators can define coverage details, including limits and deductibles. Claims The claim management module provides insurers with a highly organized way to manage all types of insurance claims, streamlining the entire lifecycle from claim filing to settlement. Users can easily filter and monitor claims by status, date, type, or amount. Additionally, they can assess key details, including claim type, risk level, policy reference, deductible, and financial status. Each claim record provides more information about the adjuster, supporting documents (such as invoices, photos, videos, and statements), and incident descriptions. Whether a claim is pending, approved, in progress, or assessed, the system enables fast, informed decisions while ensuring accuracy, traceability, and compliance at every stage. Billing The invoices dashboard provides a transparent billing experience for insurers, allowing them to easily create, manage, monitor, and track all invoices in one place. Users can filter invoices by status, insured party, amount, or due date, while viewing essential details such as invoice number, total amount, due date, and policy linkage. Each invoice record provides access to additional actions, such as viewing full details or downloading a PDF copy for record‑keeping purposes. With built‑in search, filters, and a clear layout, the dashboard streamlines financial oversight, helping ensure timely and accurate premium collections.
