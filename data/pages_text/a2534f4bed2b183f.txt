Yes No Press releases · 4 min. read · 95 reads · 30 Apr 2026 30 Apr 2026 DICEUS and InsureMO partner to accelerate insurance modernization across Europe, Middle East, and Asia-Pacific Carriers, MGAs, and brokers gain access to combined API middleware and ready-made insurance platforms, from product launch to policy administration to AI-powered customer experience. Singapore / London, April 2026 InsureMO, the global insurance API, Data, and AI Platform-as-a-Service, and DICEUS, the insurance technology company delivering ready-made platforms for policy administration, underwriting, and customer engagement, today announced a strategic technology partnership spanning Europe, the Middle East, and Asia-Pacific. The partnership addresses a persistent gap in insurance technology: carriers that modernize their middle office, including product configuration, rating, and distribution connectivity, still face months of work connecting that infrastructure to back-office systems and customer-facing digital experiences. By combining InsureMO’s API-first platform with DICEUS’s pre-built, modular software suite, the partnership gives insurers a faster path to full-stack modernization without replacing existing systems. DICEUS’s software portfolio brings practical AI capabilities across core insurance workflows. Its AI-enabled Underwriting Workbench supports intelligent submission intake and decision support, while AI-driven features extend to digital self-service experiences through its Super App and Customer Web Portal, helping insurers automate claims processes, customer support, and document handling with greater speed and efficiency. Combined with InsureMO’s AI platform, which includes Agent Builder, knowledge base, and intelligent document processing, the partnership delivers AI-ready infrastructure across underwriting, operations, and customer engagement. InsureMO’s platform powers 17,500+ insurance products configured across 500+ customers in 50+ countries, connected to 8,700+ channels and tech partners and processing $25B+ in gross written premium. DICEUS, founded in 2011, has delivered insurance technology for organisations including UNIQA, Vienna Insurance Group, Fairfax Group, and WTW, with a product suite covering policy administration, AI-powered underwriting, group insurance management, data warehousing, and mobile and web portals for policyholders and brokers. “Insurers can configure and launch products on our platform in days, but then spend months building or retrofitting the back-office and customer layers around it,” said Rajat Sharma, Chief Revenue Officer of InsureMO. “With DICEUS, we can now offer carriers a ready-made policy administration system, underwriting workbench, or digital portal that’s built to work alongside our API infrastructure. For a group insurance carrier or an MGA scaling into new lines, that changes the timeline from quarters to weeks.” Under the partnership, each company extends its client engagements to include the other’s capabilities. Carriers working with InsureMO on API infrastructure, product orchestration, or distribution can access DICEUS’s implementation-ready back-office and customer platforms. Carriers working with DICEUS on policy administration, claims, or digital engagement can connect to InsureMO’s middleware layer for product configuration, rating, and multi-channel distribution. The partnership covers all major insurance lines, including commercial, property and casualty, health, life, motor, and group/employee benefits, with particular depth in employee benefits and group insurance, where the combination of InsureMO’s product orchestration and DICEUS’s group insurance management platform addresses a segment where digital maturity remains low. “This partnership reflects our shared commitment to helping insurers modernize faster with flexible, scalable, and ready-to-implement technology,” said Illia Pinchuk, CEO of DICEUS. “Together with InsureMO, we are making it easier for insurance organisations to launch products, streamline operations, and deliver better experiences to policyholders and partners.” Insurers, MGAs, brokers, and captive managers can contact either company to explore joint modernization engagements. More at www.insuremo.com and www.diceus.com. About DICEUS DICEUS is a product-driven insurance technology company delivering ready-made software platforms and custom solutions for insurers, brokers, MGAs, and TPAs. Founded in 2011, its product suite includes policy administration, AI-powered underwriting, group insurance management, data warehousing, and digital customer and broker portals. DICEUS has delivered solutions for leading insurance organisations including UNIQA, Vienna Insurance Group, Fairfax Group, and WTW. About InsureMO InsureMO is the global insurance API, Data, and AI Platform-as-a-Service connecting insurers, distributors, and ecosystem partners through a single API layer. With thousands of atomic APIs spanning the full insurance lifecycle, InsureMO enables carriers to launch products, connect distribution channels, and build AI-powered applications at speed. The platform powers 17,500+ products configured across 500+ customers in 50+ countries, with $25B+ in gross written premium and 8,700+ channels and tech partners connected.
