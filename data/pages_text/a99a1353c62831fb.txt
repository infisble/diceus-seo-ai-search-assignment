Yes No Press releases · 3 min. read · 41 reads · 13 Jul 2026 13 Jul 2026 DICEUS partners with Kruzr to power connected motor insurance DICEUS expands its motor insurance software ecosystem through a new partnership with Kruzr, offering the possibility to add telematics and driver-safety capabilities to its insurance platform. Connected and usage-based insurance continue to reshape the motor insurance market. Many carriers already collect telematics data, but turning that data into underwriting decisions, customer engagement, and operational workflows often requires integrating multiple disconnected systems. DICEUS addresses this challenge by combining its insurance platform with telematics from Kruzr, giving insurers one connected environment instead of several standalone tools. DICEUS solutions: Policy Administration System, Underwriting Workbench, and Insurance Super App can turn driving data into action: adjusted pricing, targeted renewal offers, and cross-sell, delivered through the insurer’s customer-facing channels. Connected through Kruzr: Module that plugs into an insurer’s mobile app, detects driving behavior automatically, and rewards safer drivers with coaching, streaks, and renewal-linked perks. Through this partnership, DICEUS extends its insurance platform with telematics, enabling insurers to combine real-time driving behavior with underwriting, pricing, and customer engagement in a single ecosystem. DICEUS Policy Administration System and Underwriting Workbench can consume driving-behavior scores, trip data, and risk indicators, so underwriters and pricing teams work with continuously updated risk data instead of static assumptions. Thus, premiums, offers, and retention campaigns can reflect how a customer really drives. DICEUS customers can now combine telematics with policy administration, underwriting, and digital engagement in a single ecosystem, built on the DICEUS platform. With Kruzr embedded, insurers can unlock new use cases beyond traditional telematics, including driver-safety programs and more personalized engagement journeys. DICEUS CEO Illia Pinchuk says: “Our goal is to make the DICEUS insurance platform the place where customer engagement, telematics, and underwriting come together. Motor insurers have invested heavily in digital channels, yet most still lack meaningful engagement with customers between renewal and claim. The partnership with Kruzr is another step toward closing that gap, right inside our platform.” Pallav Singh, CEO of Kruzr: “We’re thrilled to partner with DICEUS as one of the most complete insurance platforms on the market, covering everything from underwriting to policy administration to customer engagement. Plugging Kruzr’s telematics directly into that platform means insurers don’t just get driving data. They get a system that already knows how to turn it into engagement workflows, pricing decisions, and real customer relationships. “ About DICEUS DICEUS delivers insurance platforms covering the full policy lifecycle – from underwriting to policy administration, claims, billing, analytics, and customer engagement. Founded in 2011, its product suite includes policy administration, AI-powered underwriting, group insurance management, data warehousing, digital customer and broker portals, and other solutions. DICEUS has delivered solutions to leading insurance organizations, including UNIQA, Vienna Insurance Group, Fairfax Group, The Baldwin Group, and WTW. Learn more at https://diceus.com/insurance/ About Kruzr Kruzr is a smartphone-based telematics and driver-safety platform that helps insurers and fleets reduce risk through real-time driving behavior detection, coaching, and rewards.
