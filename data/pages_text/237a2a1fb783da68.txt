Yes No Company news · 1 min. read · 320 reads · 11 Nov 2025 02 Sep 2026 DICEUS attended Insurance Innovators Summit in London On November 4-5, DICEUS’s CEO, Illia Pinchuk, and Head of Insurance Tech Solutions, Kateryna Monastyrska, attended Insurance Innovators Summit 2025 in London. During the event, our team presented DICEUS and its ready-made software products for the insurance industry, contributing to discussions about the future of the sector. Illia and Kateryna had the opportunity to meet with our customers and representatives from numerous new insurance companies and partners, including AXA, Groupe Mutuel, MetLife, Zurich Insurance, Aon, Sava, HALALI, and many more. Such events are always full of inspiring conversations and shared drive to redefine the insurance industry. Here at DICEUS, we are focused on developing innovative software for policy administration, underwriting, group insurance management, claims management, and customer engagement. Our software delivers outcomes faster, helping insurers gain a competitive edge and meet ever-changing customer expectations.
