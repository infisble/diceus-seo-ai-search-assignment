The data warehouse is the core of the BI system, which is built for data analysis and reporting. It is a blend of technologies and components which aids the strategic use of data. It is the storage of a large amount of information by a business that is designed for query and analysis. It is a process of transforming data into information and making it available to users. Advanced data warehouse with a reliable architecture that delivers an efficient workflow and information reliability thanks to automated processes. This solution provides consistent real-time control and analysis of significant bank operations. Business challenge There was a strong need to verify the information from the reports, clarify the business needs about the reporting systems, and improve the data warehouse architecture to reach a smooth and consistent workflow. The challenge was to develop and integrate a data warehouse, to implement a liquidity risk management system for cooperation with the European Bank for Reconstruction and Development. The final goal was to get a “golden source” and develop the main analytical and reporting dashboards. Technical challenges In a very short period, we had to build a new design for data warehouse architecture, the managerial reporting system. The challenge was to develop ETL processes for extracting, transforming, and loading the data from the 15 source systems to a warehouse. Solution delivered We have developed and implemented a process of regular control and analysis of the core banking operations based on Oracle UBS FLEXCUBE in partnership with the International Advisory Services Frankfurt School of Finance & Management. We built a data warehouse with 15 integrated systems, reporting systems for credit and deposit portfolios. We run multiple tests and delivered the project on time.
