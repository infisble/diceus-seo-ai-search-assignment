Yes No Company news · 2 min. read · 130 reads · 26 Mar 2026 04 Sep 2026 DICEUS attended Insurtech Insights Europe 2026 in London DICEUS visited Insurtech Insights Europe 2026, a leading event for insurance and technology professionals. Our team, including Illia Pinchuk, Kateryna Monastyrska, Dmytro Kazymir, and Oleksii Denysenko, joined industry leaders to discuss the key factors influencing the insurance sector today. Here are the things that were hard to miss: AI has moved well past the “pilot and explore” stage. It runs inside live pricing models, underwriting workflows, fraud detection, and claims-related processes. Professionals aren’t debating whether to adopt it, but rather what’s working and what isn’t. Claims are becoming a key area of competition. FNOL, instant payouts, self-service, and customer empathy are transforming claims from a back-office function into a core differentiator. However, data remains the main constraint. Without a connected data infrastructure, progress in artificial intelligence slows significantly. The industry’s gap is now between those who execute quickly and those who do not. Underwriting is quietly being rebuilt from the ground up: manual handoffs, inconsistent judgment calls, and slow triage are replaced by data-driven workflows that help make decisions faster. At DICEUS, we focus on converting these industry insights into practical solutions, including underwriting platforms, policy administration, and data and analytics layers that support real decision-making. We appreciated the meaningful conversations at the event and look forward to follow-up meetings with insurers, brokers, and MGAs.
