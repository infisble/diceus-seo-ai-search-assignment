Captive Owner Portal Captive Owner Portal, developed by DICEUS, is a software product that provides captive owners with a helicopter view of their insurance operations in one place. It includes useful modules and features for tracking premiums, policies, claims, exposure, and reinsurance. Book a demo The portal helps captive owners monitor their financials, invoices, capital, solvency, regulatory obligations, and collaborate with captive managers via centralized workflows. Once logged in, owners can view performance, financial health, claims activity, and a portfolio summary for each captive they own. AI‑driven insights, multi-captive visibility, and advanced analytics make the software streamlined and effortless for users. All dashboards are highly customizable; you can add as many metrics to track as needed. 4 reasons captive owners choose the portal Designed around how you actually work ‑ not how your captive manager's system was built. Everything you own, visible in one place If you manage more than one captive, you know the pain of piecing together a complete picture from scattered reports, spreadsheets, and manager updates. The portal solves this by consolidating your entire portfolio into a single view, comparing performance across captives, tracking cross‑captive risk exposure, and flagging issues before they escalate. You stop chasing information and start actually using it. Real‑time visibility into your financial health Capital adequacy, solvency ratios, invoices, outstanding payments, dividends - these are the numbers that keep you up at night if you can't see them clearly. The portal surfaces all of it in real time, including capital stress testing, so you can model what happens if something goes wrong. You're no longer relying on quarterly reports from your captive manager to know where you stand. Claims and risk exposure By the time a problem claim appears in a standard report, it's often already a headache. The portal gives you early warning indicators on significant claims activity, advanced loss analytics, and risk concentration analysis, so you can spot dangerous patterns while there's still time to respond. AI insights that make a difference The portal AI layer analyzes your portfolio's performance, claims patterns, underwriting results, and capital position, and surfaces the insights that actually matter. It can even recommend how to optimize capital utilization. For captive owners who don't live inside numbers every day, this is the difference between owning a captive and truly understanding it.
