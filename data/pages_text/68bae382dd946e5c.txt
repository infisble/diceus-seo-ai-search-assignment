Since 2011, we have accomplished more than 150 projects, helping SMBs and enterprises overcome business challenges with the right technology solutions. About the Role We are looking for a new-generation Product Designer who combines strong UX/product thinking with AI-assisted prototyping and low-code development. This is not a traditional UI/UX design role focused on manually creating individual screens in Figma. We need someone who can take a business problem or a set of requirements, understand the underlying workflow, design the product experience, and rapidly turn it into a high-quality interactive or functional solution using modern AI and low-code tools. The primary domain is B2B insurance software: policy administration, underwriting, claims, billing, captive insurance, and other insurance operations.
