Yes No Company news · 1 min. read · 1118 reads · 13 Nov 2023 09 Apr 2025 DICEUS CEO attended the WTW Airport Conference and visited the Lloyd’s building We are thrilled to share that DICEUS CEO Illia Pinchuk has visited WTW Airport Conference 2023 in London on November 8-10. This event was the fourth annual Airport Risk Community (ARC) hosted conference. The main aim was to bring airport owners, operators, and partners together from across the globe to collaborate and discuss the challenges faced by the industry. WTW collaborates with the aviation industry to better understand all the risks and provide the most effective solutions. Our team has felt like a part of the community connecting aviation professionals globally to share their knowledge, expertise, and tailored solutions. Thanks to Vyacheslav Andriyko, Illia was also lucky to have visited Lloyd’s building in London, which is the center of Lloyd’s global operations. It is a global market for insurance and reinsurance specialists, known as a London icon. It was a real pleasure to explore more about the marketplace and its history. DICEUS and Illia are grateful to Simon Aubrey Jones, Darren Porter, and John Rooley for their warm hospitality.
