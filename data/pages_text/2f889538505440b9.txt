Yes No Company news · 1 min. read · 523 reads · 31 Jan 2025 05 Mar 2026 DICEUS visited ITC London 2025 On January 27-28, 2025, the DICEUS team—represented by Illia Pinchuk, Kateryna Monastyrska, and Dmytro Kazymir—attended ITC London, an event dedicated to exploring the latest trends and innovations in the insurance industry. We were excited to connect with industry leaders and present our cutting-edge insurance technology solutions, including a mobile app, chatbot, and web portal designed to streamline operations and enhance customer experiences. A special thanks to the incredible companies we had the pleasure of engaging with, including Unipol Assicurazioni, Zurich Insurance, Celent, MetLife UK, Always Vacation Co., Chubb, CLARK, UNIQA Insurance Group Liechtenstein, Sumitomo Mitsui Banking Corporation – SMBC Group, Beazley, Groupe Mutuel, Trawick International, Pausable, SureInsure Insurance Advisors, INZMO, Marsh, and many more. We truly appreciate the insightful discussions, valuable feedback, and exciting opportunities that emerged during the event. Looking forward to continuing these conversations and shaping the future of insurance technology together!
