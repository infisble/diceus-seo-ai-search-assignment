Yes No Company news · 2 min. read · 464 reads · 27 Oct 2025 19 Feb 2026 Super App recognized as one of the Best Customer Engagement Software DICEUS Super App was recognized as one of the best customer engagement software for the insurance industry at the Global Insurtech Awards 2025. This recognition means a lot to us as it proves we are on the right path. Super App was developed for companies offering health, life, unit-linked, car, and travel insurance to solve their real problems: high call center workloads, customer churn, and outdated technology. Our solution is modular, mobile-first, and allows companies to launch only the functionalities they need while providing a seamless omnichannel experience for their customers. It empowers insurers to unify multiple business lines into one intuitive mobile experience, enhance policyholder loyalty, and increase sales by enabling customers to quote, buy, and renew policies directly in the app. Our CEO, Illia Pinchuk, who leads Super App product development, believes that customer-facing tools must be flexible, scalable, and developed around real customer journeys. We are thankful to the Global InsurTech Awards team and jury for this recognition and are committed to making our insurtech software products even better.
