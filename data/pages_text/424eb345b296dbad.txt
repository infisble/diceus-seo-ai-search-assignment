Yes No Company news · 1 min. read · 368 reads · 16 Sep 2024 01 Apr 2025 DICEUS became a Google Cloud Partner in the Partner Advantage Region UK and Ireland September 12, 2024 – DICEUS became a Google Cloud Platform Partner in the Partner Advantage Region UK and Ireland. DICEUS now has access to various tools, resources, and training to use Google Cloud for its customers and business. This partnership program allows its participants to provide consulting services concerning Google Cloud, Google Workspace, and Chrome. Additionally, DICEUS is eligible to develop integrated solutions for Google Cloud or Google Workspace. “We value this partnership and think it will bring more capacity and help handle big volumes of information for our customers, having full confidence that Google Cloud solutions are secure and manageable,” – says DICEUS CEO Illia Pinchuk. Our services related to Google Cloud Platform are vast, comprising those related to storage, databases, networking, operations, data analytics, and more. These include the following: Cloud transformation and migration Data migration and DWH development Data analytics API gateways Cloud app development (native mobile apps) We use the latest Google Cloud SDKs, tools, resources, and our industry background (insurance, banking, healthcare, retail, etc.) to deliver the most effective solutions for our customers.
