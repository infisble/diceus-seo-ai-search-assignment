Yes No Company news · 2 min. read · 3548 reads · 29 Jan 2020 24 Sep 2026 DICEUS is a sponsoring partner at the 4th annual retail banking technologies summit in Vienna We are thrilled to become an official sponsoring partner to the 4th annual Retail Banking Technologies Summit that will take place in Vienna on 4-6 February 2020. By attending the event, we aim to showcase our expertise in software solutions development for banking, share our experience in enterprise software development, and demonstrate the latest business cases accomplished by the DICEUS team. Retail Banking Technologies Summit is a good starting point both for buyers and solution providers to reach out to the right people. The event will host speakers from market-leading banks, FinTechs, and financial Startups. Visitors will have the opportunity to learn about the latest frameworks and best practices associated with retail banking, open banking, APIs, and payments. Digital transformation and technology innovation will be actively discussed at the summit, as well. Take a look at the most exciting talk topics of the conference: “Adapting for an Open Banking World”, “Customer Experience as a Competitive Advantage”, and “Regulatory Compliance and Cybersecurity”. This year the attendees will speak mainly about open APIs, customer experience, FinTech partnerships, and payments innovation as these subjects for discussion completely display the recent technology trends for the global banking industry. About DICEUS DICEUS harnesses its decade of software engineering experience in the banking niche to provide solutions that keep up to date. Working with banks across the world, our teams have garnered great international expertise in retail and corporate banking. Today, we are ready to share our knowledge and provide our skills and competencies to build intelligent banking solutions that address the toughest needs of our customers. Feel free to reach out to our experts to discuss your software needs for banking. Check out our services for banks to get familiar with real business cases and our solutions.
