Yes No Business · Insurance · 1 min. read · 352 reads · 16 Jul 2026 03 Aug 2026 DICEUS CEO Illia Pinchuk on the Insurtech Leadership Podcast Recently, Illia Pinchuk, CEO and founder of DICEUS, joined Joshua R. Hollander for a new episode of the Insurtech Leadership Podcast. The conversation was about a problem familiar to anyone running a captive or captive management firm: growth that depends on adding people, not improving systems. Where spreadsheets stop scaling Illia Pinchuk walks through why captive managers relying on spreadsheets eventually hit a ceiling. Every new policy, renewal, and regulatory filing adds manual work. Growth stops being a matter of strategy and becomes a matter of headcount. Where value leaks out The episode also covers the less visible cost of manual captive management – the value that disappears in disconnected systems, delayed reporting, and processes that depend on one person remembering how things work. Pricing built around captive needs A central part of the discussion is feature-based pricing, and why it matters in a segment where cost efficiency is the entire reason a captive exists in the first place.
