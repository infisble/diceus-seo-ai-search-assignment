Yes No Company news · 1 min. read · 313 reads · 16 May 2024 16 May 2024 DICEUS visited Tonik 2024 in Torshavn, Faroe Islands On May 3, our Head of Growth, Dmytro Kazymir, and Regional Sales Manager in the Faroe Islands and Iceland, Oddur Friang Rasmussen, attended the Tonik 2024 event in Torshavn, Faroe Islands. The DICEUS team met many representatives from various industries, government organizations, and tech startups and was able to present DICEUS services and expertise. The Faroe Islands boast breathtakingly rugged landscapes and extraordinarily welcoming people, creating a uniquely inviting and picturesque experience for every visitor. We are thankful to the Tonik organizers for the chance to attend this event, listen to interesting speeches, and meet our partners. We look forward to continuing the dialog and collaborations with local businesses.
