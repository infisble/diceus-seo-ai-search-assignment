Yes No Company news · 2 min. read · 477 reads · 16 Sep 2024 05 Mar 2026 DICEUS attended Global Connect 2024 On September 11, the DICEUS team attended Global Connect 2024 in Zurich, where DICEUS CEO Illia Pinchuk took the stage to present the family of software products for insurance . The presentation highlighted the innovative mobile app and chatbot we developed for the insurance sector. Over this insightful day, we engaged with many industry leaders, including Aon, Zurich Insurance Group, Swiss Re, Vaudoise, Groupe Mutuel, and NN Group. These connections are invaluable as we explore opportunities to innovate and lead in the insurance technology space. Thanks to our partners and organizers of the event Swiss Insurtech Hub, with special recognition to Silvia Signoretti, Sharan Kaur, Brijesh Luthra, Roberta Profeta, and Pawel Konieczniak. We look forward to continuing these valuable conversations and exploring new opportunities in the industry. About DICEUS DICEUS is a software product-led company delivering cutting-edge tech solutions for the insurance industry. DICEUS software products offer seamless integration with various messaging platforms, open data portals, and payment systems, providing omnichannel customer experience and exceptional performance. In addition to offering products, DICEUS serves as an integrator, developing custom software solutions that cater to the specific needs of insurance businesses. These include core systems, DWH, mobile apps, chatbots, customer web portals, and more. Since 2011, we’ve completed many projects for companies such as UNIQA, VIG, Fairfax Group, and more. Our industry partners include Fadata and BriteCore. About Global Connect Global Connect is a unique event organized by Swiss Insurtech Hub, where the representatives of the insurtech companies can communicate with leaders from global insurers on an exclusive lake cruise, sharing their innovations and participating in round table discussions.
