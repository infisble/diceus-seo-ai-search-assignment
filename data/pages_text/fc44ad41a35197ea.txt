Yes No Press releases · 2 min. read · 516 reads · 14 Dec 2023 14 Dec 2023 OSCE Parliamentary Assembly and DICEUS established a partnership for website advancement November 2023 — The OSCE Parliamentary Assembly (OSCE PA) started collaborating with DICEUS, a custom software development company, to migrate its website from Content Management System Joomla version 3 to the latest version. Since 1990, the OSCE Parliamentary Assembly has worked hard on streamlining inter-parliamentary dialogue within 57 countries of Eurasia and North America. The organization is a regional structure under the Charter of the United Nations that plays an instrumental role in early conflict warning and prevention, risk management, and post-crisis rehabilitation worldwide. In today’s digital world, the challenge of OSCE PA is bringing together 323 parliamentarians from Europe, Asia, and North America and ensuring forums for inter-parliamentary communication, election observation, and strengthening global collaboration on economic, political, security, environmental, and human rights challenges. Understanding the need for a professional tech partner, OSCE PA chose DICEUS as a skilled vendor to execute its CMS migration project. As a result, DICEUS implemented the project focusing on quality and cost-effectiveness. The DICEUS Team updated website modules and extensions seamlessly with nearly zero downtime. Today, DICEUS continues to provide maintenance and support, including website speed optimization services. OSCE PA and DICEUS look forward to a continued partnership and remain committed to improving the vision of human security through effective digital solutions.
