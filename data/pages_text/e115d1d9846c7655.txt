Yes No Company news · 1 min. read · 2088 reads · 25 Sep 2024 05 Mar 2026 DICEUS presented its software products during Italian Insurtech Roadshow in Milan The DICEUS Team, CEO Illia Pinchuk and Head of Sales and Marketing Kateryna Monastyrska participated in the Italian InsurTech Roadshow in Milan on September 18-19. A range of insurtech-related events was organized in collaboration with our partner, Swiss Insurtech Hub. Illia presented a family of innovative software products and illustrated how they could revolutionize how insurers interact with their customers, provide services, and scale up sales. Special attention was paid to Super App and Chatbot, as these can efficiently help insurers decrease operational costs, enhance customer loyalty, and increase revenue. The event featured dynamic pitching sessions for companies such as Zurich Insurance Italy, UnipolSai, Vittoria Hub, and G-gravity. Illia and Kateryna also visited Composable Insurance’s Round Table and Startup Pitch Session, which were dedicated to innovation in the insurance sector. At Mia Platform, they were able not only to pitch but also to listen to insights from professionals working for Mia-FinTech, Reale Group, AXA, Helvetia Insurance Italy, and others. Thanks to the roadshow organizers and participants for the opportunity to engage with industry leaders and introduce our software solutions.
