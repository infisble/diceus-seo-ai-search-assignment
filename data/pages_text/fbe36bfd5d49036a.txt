Yes No Company news · 1 min. read · 401 reads · 25 Sep 2024 09 Apr 2025 DICEUS visited Insurtech UK AGM in London DICEUS’s Head of Growth, Dmytro Kazymir, visited the Insurtech UK AGM on September 24 at the London office of Insurtech UK Principal Partner McKinsey & Co. The goal of the Annual General Meeting was to inform Insurtech UK partners and members about updates and nominations. It was insightful to listen to various panel discussions and learn more about the state of the UK insurtech sector. Thanks for speeches from Pietro Richelli (Associate Partner, McKinsey), Louise Birritteri (CEO & Founder, Pikl), Tsubasa Hotta (VP & GM International, Tractable), and Leda Zaharieva (Partner, McKinsey). DICEUS is grateful to Melissa Collett, CEO of Insurtech UK, for organizing this meeting and warm welcome. It is always great to connect with the Insurtech UK members and see the community growing!
