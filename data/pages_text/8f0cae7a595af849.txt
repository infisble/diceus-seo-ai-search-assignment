IT infrastructure management services encompass the strategies required to design, monitor, and manage IT resources. An example of infrastructure management is cloud-based storage and backup solutions. What is the function of infrastructure management? The primary function of infrastructure management is to optimize IT resources and ensure seamless, uninterrupted business operations. Infrastructure management strategy involves planning and implementing best practices to manage an organization’s IT assets effectively.
