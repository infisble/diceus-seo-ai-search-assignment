Yes No Company news · 1 min. read · 179 reads · 12 Feb 2026 12 Feb 2026 DICEUS visited the Central Bank of Jordan Recently, DICEUS representatives in Jordan — Ramzi Haddad, Coordinator for the Middle East and South Africa, and Diana Haddad, Regional Manager for the Middle East and South Africa — held a meeting with the Central Bank of Jordan (CBJ). The discussion focused on potential cooperation to implement advanced scientific and technological solutions in the Bank’s operations. The parties explored financial sector trends, future development prospects, and the role of innovation in improving efficiency and security. Both DICEUS and the Central Bank of Jordan expressed confidence that these initiatives will strengthen our partnership and create opportunities to introduce innovative technologies into the country’s banking sector.
