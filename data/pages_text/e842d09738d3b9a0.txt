Contact us Trends in AI development: Glimpses of the future As a forward-looking IT vendor, DICEUS tries to stay ahead of the technological curve, keeping track of the emerging trends in the niche. Democratization of AI At its arrival, any new know-how is a big-ticket item, requiring highly specialized expertise for handling. Over time, it undergoes democratization: it becomes more affordable, with many users learning its ropes. This progress pattern is characteristic of artificial intelligence as well, and the advent of ChatGPT was a visible marker of such a shift. Today, any person without technical training can utilize it to generate synthetic texts and images free of charge, while for organizations, the low subscription fee is a good bargain. Evidently, other AI-powered technologies are likely to follow suit one of these days, becoming available for non-tech personnel of small businesses and startups on short commons, who will leverage their enormous potential in multiple professional use cases. Multimodal AI AI solutions will move from their current ability to process and generate only one kind of data to multimodal principles when models fueling them allow AI tools to handle texts, video, audio, and images within one input and produce content containing all of them in the output. iPhones can already perform object recognition on photos by analyzing images, metadata, and search data. Other AI systems are likely to embrace this function pretty soon. Agentic AI AI tools are becoming more independent in doing their jobs. They will gradually turn into totally autonomous AI agents that can perceive the environment, register changes in it in real-time, make conclusions, plan their actions, execute them, and analyze the results, constantly learning from each interaction thanks to feedback loops. Moreover, being honed to solve specific assignments, they will be able to cooperate in dealing with more complex tasks, radically streamlining and facilitating network diagnostics, fraud detection, risk evaluation, and customer service in different industries. AI-assisted software development Large chunks of software code are repetitive but necessary elements of the program, so developers spend a lot of time reproducing them over and over again. The SDLC proceeds at least 25% faster and error-free when code generation tools like Copilot by Github come into play. The most disruptive platforms used as software development assistants can not only understand syntax, consider context, and anticipate code lines, completing them. They can also be employed for code review and optimization, bug detection and resolution, quality assurance, etc., and over time, the scope of AI functionalities in this area will only expand. Gartner predicts that next year, close to a third of new apps will have AI-generated UI, whereas in 2027, 15% of such products globally will be produced by AI without human participation. Ethical AI development and compliance As the role of AI across multiple spheres enhances, people take thought for its ethical usage, which presupposes delineating accountability zones between AI developers and users, ensuring transparent and unbiased operation of AI models, and guaranteeing privacy and security of data employed for model training. Seeing that many people still feel a distrust towards artificial intelligence (in the USA, this number amounts to 70%), governments have become concerned about AI ethics, too, issuing stringent regulations. So far, such laws (GDPR, HIPAA, CCPA, and others) are mostly related to data security and cover any digital products; however, the legal framework is expanding to include AI initiatives specifically. In May 2024, the EU adopted the Artificial Intelligence Act that establishes a dedicated AI Office at the European Commission that will issue decrees concerning limitations and checks of high-risk AI systems. The US federal legislation lags behind in this domain, so state governments have stepped into the breach. And the harbinger of future changes is the Colorado AI Act, passed by the state in the same May. We will definitely see other similar laws implemented by local and national governments soon.
