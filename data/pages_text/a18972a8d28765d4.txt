Illia Pinchuk Founder and CEO About Illia Illia Pinchuk is Founder and CEO of DICEUS, a product-led company delivering ready-made insurance software solutions. Under his leadership, DICEUS has built a portfolio spanning the full insurance value chain, including the Alternative Risk Operating Platform, AI-Powered Underwriting Workbench, Captive Management Platform, Captive Owner Portal, Risk Retention Group Platform, Group Insurance Management Platform, Insurance General Ledger Platform, Data Warehouse for Insurance, Reinsurance Platform, Broker Portal, and customer-facing solutions like the Super App, Customer Web Portal, and Chatbot. DICEUS configurable products help insurers, underwriters, MGAs, captive managers, risk retention groups, brokers, and other alternative risk organizations modernize operations, improve underwriting efficiency, automate policy and financial processes, and deliver stronger digital experiences. Since 2011, Illia has guided DICEUS in partnering with top insurers and brokers such as The Baldwin Group, Fairfax Group, WTW, UNIQA, and VIG, along with insurance-focused organizations including Fadata, VCIA, Insurtech UK, Swiss Insurtech Hub, InsureMO, Claim Technology, and Blink Payment, and technology leaders Microsoft, Google Cloud, and Oracle.
