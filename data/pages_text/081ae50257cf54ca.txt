Yes No Company news · 1 min. read · 55 reads · 26 May 2026 26 May 2026 DICEUS attended Risk-!n Conference in Zurich On May 21-22, the DICEUS Team, Illia Pinchuk, CEO, and Kateryna Monastyrska, Head of Insurance Technology Solutions, participated in the Risk-!n Conference in Zurich. The conference gathered leaders from well-known companies such as Allianz, Aon, AXA, Munich Re, Marsh, Swiss Re, Chubb, Roche, ABB, Airbus, Philip Morris International, and Nestlé. It provided a comfortable platform for practical discussions on captives, risk operations, compliance, resilience, AI, governance, and underwriting transformation. Key topics that were discussed included continuous reliance on spreadsheets and manual reporting, limited visibility across risk and financial operations, fragmented underwriting workflows, and modernization challenges. We at DICEUS align with these areas and addresses challenges through our software solutions: Captive Management Platform, Captive Owner Portal, and AI-Powered Underwriting Workbench.
