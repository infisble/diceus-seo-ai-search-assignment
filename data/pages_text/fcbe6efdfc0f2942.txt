Requirements and product-decision work is currently happening on this project — epics with data models, acceptance criteria, and Product knowledge docs exist — but ownership of that work is fragmented. Engineers are sometimes the ones raising and closing the gap themselves on core business questions (e.g., SLA/escalation logic, multi-approver rules) that should be owned by a BA, and several open product decisions have sat unresolved for months. This role exists to give that work a single, accountable owner across the full lifecycle — from client requirements to engineering-ready specs — and to actually close the backlog of open decisions rather than let them accumulate.
