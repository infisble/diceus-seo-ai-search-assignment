Yes No Company news · 1 min. read · 228 reads · 19 Jan 2026 19 Jan 2026 DICEUS team attended the CEE Forum in Vienna On January 13–14, DICEUS CEO Illia Pinchuk and Head of Insurance Technology Solutions Kateryna Monastyrska attended the Central and Eastern European Forum (CEE Forum) in Vienna. It is one of the key regional events for insurance and banking leaders across Central and Eastern Europe. A key idea of the discussions with banks and insurers at the forum was the following: the question is no longer whether to modernize, but how to do it without disrupting daily operations. Many conversations highlighted the same priorities: step-by-step core modernization instead of “Big Bang” programs, analytical data platforms (DWH) that support ALM, risk, and regulatory reporting, moving AI from pilots into production within real workflows, and scaling digital platforms without adding operational complexity. These topics closely align with how DICEUS supports financial institutions today: modular core components, production-ready data platforms, and AI-ready architectures built for execution and measurable results. We thank the CEE Forum community for the practical discussions and strong regional perspective. If you are exploring core modernization, DWH, or applied AI in banking or insurance, we would be glad to connect and share concrete examples of our solutions in practice.
